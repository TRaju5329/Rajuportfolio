KharchuLu – Account & Data Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your KharchuLu account and permanently remove all your data associated with it, you may request deletion at any time.

How to Request Data Deletion

Send an email to the address below with the subject:

"Delete My KharchuLu Account & Data"

Include the following details in your message:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your Firebase Account ID (optional)

Send request here:

Email: frontenddeveloperraju@gmail.com

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon request, we will delete:

  • All expense & income entries
  • All budgets, categories, reminders
  • All cloud backup data stored in Firebase
  • Your account login & authentication record

Retention and Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 working days. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

If you have questions, contact us anytime at the same email.
