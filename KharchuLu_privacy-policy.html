KharchuLu – Privacy Policy

Personal finance & expense tracker app
Last Updated: 22 November 2025

KharchuLu (“the App”, “we”, “our”, “us”) is a personal finance application that helps you track expenses, incomes, investments, budgets, and recurring transactions. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using KharchuLu, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

Section 1

Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We do not require you to create an online account and we do not ask for:

  • Your full name
  • Home address
  • Aadhaar / PAN / government ID numbers
  • Bank account numbers
  • Credit or debit card details

The only personal information we may receive is your email address if you contact us for support or feedback.

1.2 Financial Data Entered by You

KharchuLu stores data that you manually enter, including:

  • Expense records
  • Income records
  • Investment records
  • Categories and notes
  • Budgets and limits
  • Recurring transaction rules

This data is stored locally on your device. It is not automatically uploaded to our servers.

1.3 Device & Technical Data

To improve stability and performance, we may collect non-personal technical information such as:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • Anonymous crash logs and error reports

This information, if collected (e.g. via crash reporting tools), is used purely for debugging and improving the app.

Section 2

How Your Data is Stored

All financial data you enter in KharchuLu is stored locally on your device using local storage technologies (for example, local databases or persistent storage mechanisms).

We do not automatically upload your financial data to any remote server owned by us. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, the locally stored information will be removed from your device.

Important: Since your financial data is stored only on your device by default, we strongly recommend using the export feature (CSV / PDF) regularly if you want to keep backups.
Section 3

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data with any third party.

  • We do not share your expense, income, or investment details with advertisers.
  • We do not send your financial records to analytics companies.
  • We do not share your financial data with payment gateways or external services.

Any data that leaves your device does so only when you explicitly export or share it (for example, as a CSV or PDF file).

Section 4

How We Use Your Data (Inside the App)

All of the financial data you enter is used within the app only to provide features such as:

  • Showing daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly totals
  • Calendar view where you can see how much you spent or invested on a particular day
  • Viewing, editing, updating, or deleting transactions for any date
  • Generating analytics like category-wise charts and line trends
  • Managing budgets and showing warnings when you cross your limit
  • Handling recurring transactions according to rules you configure

These calculations and views are generated on your device based on the data you have entered.

Section 5

Budget Warnings & Notifications

KharchuLu allows you to set budgets for different time ranges, such as:

  • Daily budgets
  • Monthly budgets
  • Yearly budgets
  • Custom range budgets

When your spending crosses the budget limit you configured:

  • The budget bar on the home screen may turn red to indicate you are over budget.
  • When you are within your budget, the bar may show as green.
  • You may receive warning messages or notifications (for example, every 5 minutes) to remind you that your budget has been exceeded, depending on your settings.

These budget calculations and alerts are processed locally on your device.

Section 6

Data Export (CSV / PDF)

KharchuLu provides an option to export your transactions and summaries as:

  • CSV (Comma-Separated Values) files
  • PDF reports

Export happens only when you manually request it from within the app. Exported files are saved on your device or to a location you choose (for example, cloud storage or email), and are not automatically sent to us.

Section 7

Permissions We Use

Depending on your device and Android version, the app may request the following permissions:

7.1 Storage / Files

  • To export CSV or PDF files and save them on your device.
  • To read or write the exported files when you choose to open or share them.

7.2 Notifications

  • To show budget warnings when you cross your set limits.
  • To remind you about recurring transactions or important events, if enabled.

We do not request access to your contacts, precise location, microphone, or camera for the core app functionality.

Section 8

Children’s Privacy

KharchuLu is intended for general use and is not specifically targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Section 9

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or in applicable laws. When we do, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
