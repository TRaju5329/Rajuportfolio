KharchuLu – Privacy Policy

Personal finance & expense tracker app
Last Updated: 22 November 2025

KharchuLu (“the App”, “we”, “our”, “us”) is a personal finance application that helps you track expenses, incomes, investments, budgets, and recurring transactions. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using KharchuLu, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

Section 1

Information We Collect

1.1 Account & Contact Information

When you sign up or use certain features, we may collect basic account information such as:

  • Your display name
  • Your email address
  • A user ID generated by our authentication system (for example, Firebase Auth UID)

We do not ask for or store:

  • Home address
  • Aadhaar / PAN / other government ID numbers
  • Bank account numbers
  • Credit or debit card numbers
  • UPI PINs or other payment passwords

If you contact us for support or feedback, we may also receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

1.2 Financial Data Entered by You

KharchuLu stores data that you manually enter, including:

  • Expense records
  • Income records
  • Investment records
  • Categories and notes
  • Budgets and limits
  • Recurring transaction rules

This data is stored locally on your device and may also be synced to a secure cloud database (for example, Google Firebase Firestore) so that your records can be backed up and accessed across devices when you sign in.

1.3 Device & Technical Data

To improve stability and performance, we may collect non-personal technical information such as:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • Anonymous crash logs and error reports

This information, if collected (for example via crash reporting tools), is used purely for debugging and improving the app.

1.4 Subscription & Premium Status

If you upgrade to premium features, we may store simple subscription information in your user profile, such as:

  • Current subscription status (for example, free or active)
  • Subscription type or plan (for example, manual or other labels)
  • The date and time when your subscription was last updated

Payments are handled by third-party providers (such as UPI apps, PhonePe, GPay, etc.). KharchuLu does not process or store your card numbers, UPI PIN, or bank login details.

Section 2

How Your Data is Stored

2.1 Local Storage on Your Device

Your financial data is stored locally on your device using local storage technologies (for example, local databases or persistent storage mechanisms). If you uninstall the app or clear its data, the locally stored information will be removed from your device.

2.2 Cloud Storage (Firebase)

When you sign in or use cloud features, some or all of your data (for example, your transactions, categories, and subscription status) may be stored in a secure cloud database such as Google Firebase Firestore. This allows your data to be backed up and synced between devices.

Access to this cloud data is tied to your account. Only you (when signed in) and authorized backend systems can access it. We may occasionally view limited information (such as your subscription status or email) to provide support or manage premium access, but we do not sell or share your financial data with advertisers.

Important: Even if your data is synced to the cloud, we recommend exporting your data regularly (CSV / PDF) if you want to keep additional backups under your own control.
Section 3

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data with any third party.

  • We do not share your expense, income, or investment details with advertisers.
  • We do not send your financial records to marketing companies.

3.1 Service Providers (e.g., Firebase)

We may use trusted third-party service providers to run certain parts of the app, such as:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, database, and cloud messaging
  • Notification libraries for local reminders

These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy policies and security standards. We use them only to operate and improve the app, not to sell your data.

Any data that leaves your device directly under your control does so only when you explicitly export or share it (for example, as a CSV or PDF file).

Section 4

How We Use Your Data (Inside the App)

All of the financial data you enter is used within the app (and, when applicable, within our backend services) to provide features such as:

  • Showing daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly totals
  • Calendar view where you can see how much you spent or invested on a particular day
  • Viewing, editing, updating, or deleting transactions for any date
  • Generating analytics like category-wise charts and line trends
  • Managing budgets and showing warnings when you cross your limit
  • Handling recurring transactions according to rules you configure
  • Determining whether certain premium features (for example, calendar view or exports) are available for your account

These calculations and views are generated using your data stored on your device and, when you are signed in, data kept in the cloud database.

Section 5

Budget Warnings & Notifications

KharchuLu allows you to set budgets for different time ranges, such as:

  • Daily budgets
  • Monthly budgets
  • Yearly budgets
  • Custom range budgets

When your spending crosses the budget limit you configured:

  • The budget bar on the home screen may turn red to indicate you are over budget.
  • When you are within your budget, the bar may show as green.
  • You may receive warning messages or notifications to remind you that your budget has been exceeded, depending on your settings.

KharchuLu may also send reminders (for example, daily reminders to add your expenses) using local notifications on your device and/or cloud messaging services such as Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Section 6

Data Export (CSV / PDF)

KharchuLu provides an option to export your transactions and summaries as:

  • CSV (Comma-Separated Values) files
  • PDF reports

Export happens only when you manually request it from within the app. Exported files are saved on your device or to a location you choose (for example, cloud storage or email), and are not automatically sent to us.

Section 7

Permissions We Use

Depending on your device and Android version, the app may request the following permissions:

7.1 Storage / Files

  • To export CSV or PDF files and save them on your device.
  • To read or write the exported files when you choose to open or share them.

7.2 Notifications

  • To show budget warnings when you cross your set limits.
  • To remind you about recurring transactions or to log your daily expenses and income.
  • To deliver push notifications via services such as Firebase Cloud Messaging (for example, general updates or reminders), if enabled.

7.3 Internet

  • To sync your data with the cloud database (for example, Firebase Firestore) when you are signed in.
  • To receive push notifications from our backend or from Firebase Cloud Messaging.

We do not request access to your contacts, precise location, microphone, or camera for the core app functionality.

Section 8

Children’s Privacy

KharchuLu is intended for general use and is not specifically targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Section 9

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or in applicable laws. When we do, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
